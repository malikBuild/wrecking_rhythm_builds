How to play

  1. Tap anywhere to drop the wrecking ball. It falls straight down onto the orange ring.
  2. Only hits that land on a heavy beat smash: the big drum and power chords, the tall orange bars at the top. The fall takes a moment, so tap a little early.
  3. PERFECT breaks 3 floors, GOOD breaks 1. Steel floors only break on PERFECT.
  4. A miss costs a ball. Clear all towers to start the next wave and win a ball back.
  5. Combos multiply your score up to ×8. Your best 5 runs are saved on your phone.

On your phone

  • Sound on: this is a rhythm game. On iPhone, switch off silent mode (the side switch), or you'll hear nothing.
  • Hold the phone upright (portrait).
  • Use the phone speaker or wired headphones. Bluetooth headphones add a delay.
  • Hits feel early or late? Open SETTINGS → CALIBRATE and tap along with the big drum 8 times. Your phone remembers it.
  • Fullscreen, like an app: on iPhone, in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android, in Chrome tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen or Install app.
  • The first load downloads about 10 MB. After that it starts fast.
